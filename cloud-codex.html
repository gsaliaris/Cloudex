Classification follows the World Meteorological Organization’s ten cloud genera plus a handful of rare and high-atmosphere forms. On a phone, the capture button opens your camera; on a laptop it opens a file picker. Photos are downscaled and stored locally in this browser. Live conditions come from the National Weather Service (US stations) and Open-Meteo.com (worldwide, CC BY 4.0); when neither can be reached, sample conditions are shown.
